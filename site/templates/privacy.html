The short version: this site doesn't track you.

This is a static site. Every page is a plain HTML file, and nothing on it runs in your browser to watch what you do.

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
  • No third-party requests. Styles, images and fonts all come from this site or from your own device.
  • No forms, accounts or sign-ups.

The web server and Cloudflare, which sits in front of it, keep standard access logs (IP address, time and page requested) for a short time, for security and troubleshooting. The site doesn't use them to track you.

Each project linked from here lives on its own site and has its own privacy policy. Once you follow a link to a project, that policy is the one that applies.
